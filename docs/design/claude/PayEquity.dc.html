Are we paying Germany fairly?

We compare each person's base pay with the middle of the pay band for their role, level and country. That ratio is the compa-ratio: 1.00 is the middle, below 0.80 is under the band, above 1.20 is over it.

Is any team paid below its bands?

Yes. Customer Success has a median compa-ratio of 0.92, so the whole team sits about 8% under the middle of its bands.
